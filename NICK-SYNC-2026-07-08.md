## Where the app side landed (last 48h)

Scout is now the center tab and the landing screen, and it works end to end
by voice on both phones:

- **Talks and acts**: draft outreach (incl. hiring managers) runs on the
  draft-job pipeline with live progress cards; auto-apply goes through a
  scope receipt; job hunting ("find me a PM role to apply to") matches the
  feed and applies with one confirm; browse ("show me people at Goldman")
  returns people cards from a new search-only path; replies to spoken asks
  are read aloud.
- **The trust ladder** (all field-tested this week): firm/role mishear
  autocorrect → did-you-mean → pre-flight coach → refine card → chat. Plus
  a rolling conversation memory (answers inherit company/count from earlier
  turns) and a **spend gate**: voice asks that cost credits confirm by
  default, transcription-garbage targets always confirm.
- **Two server pieces we built that you should know about**:
  - `POST /api/mobile/scout/ask` — a v0 of YOUR translator. Handles
    `find_contacts` (search-only preview, no emails, uncharged) and
    `draft_outreach` (returns jobRef per the contract), plus an
    `action='classify'` LLM intent classifier (gpt-4o-mini, cached 14d)
    that the app uses for any phrasing the fast-path regexes can't parse.
  - `GET /api/mobile/scout/active` — re-hydration, already reconciled in
    the doc.

None of this is precious — it's scaffolding shaped like the contract so the
app could ship while you build. Your brain replaces the classifier and the
translator internals; the envelope stays.
